# Code of Conduct

Envoy contributors and maintainers agree to make participation respectful, constructive, and free from harassment.

## Expected Behavior

- Be considerate in technical discussion and review.
- Focus feedback on ideas, code, and observable behavior.
- Respect different experience levels, backgrounds, and communication styles.
- Protect private information shared for maintenance or security work.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing, or sharing private
information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement And Reporting

Maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, or restrict participation when this code is violated. Report a
concern privately to a repository owner through GitHub. If no private contact is available, open a minimal issue asking
for one without publishing incident details. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
